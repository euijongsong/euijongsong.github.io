I am a Ph.D. student in the integrated M.S.–Ph.D. program in Statistics at Seoul National University, advised by Gunwoong Park in the Causal AI Lab. I work on causal discovery and structural causal models.

Before joining SNU in 2024, I studied life sciences at Konkuk University.

brejsong@snu.ac.kr Google Scholar GitHub

Research

My research concerns when structural causal models are identifiable beyond additive noise assumptions, and how to learn them with deep learning. I also work on outlier-robust causal discovery for high-dimensional linear structural equation models.
