Euijong Song 송의종

I’m a Ph.D. student in statistics at Seoul National University and a member of the Causal AI Lab, where I’m advised by Professor Gunwoong Park.

My research focuses on making non-identifiable statistical objects identifiable under realistic assumptions. I study structural causal models beyond additive noise and develop deep-learning methods for causal discovery.

Recently, I’ve also been interested in identifiability in measurement error models.
